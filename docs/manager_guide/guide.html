Manager Guide

What you do in the Pumpvision app, and when.

Credit SaleWhen a credit customer takes fuel and the attendant has not entered it. Enter it straight away and get the signed slip.
PaymentEvery time a customer pays money you receive: cash, cheque or bank transfer.
ExpenseEvery expense paid from the pump's cash, the same day.
Lube SaleEach oil, grease or coolant sale, cash or credit.
Everything you enter is saved with your name and the time. The owner sees it at once and nothing can be deleted from the app, so check the figures before you press the button.

Signing in and the Home screen

  • Today's tasks: a green tick means done. "Expenses logged today" opens the expense form. "Previous shift readings" and "Paytm report" only show whether the owner's side is done; you do not need to act on them.
  • Pending action: bank transfers you logged that the owner has not confirmed yet.
  • Keep your password private. Everything entered under your name is your responsibility.

1. Credit Sale

Credit tab at the bottom, or the Credit Sale button on Home.

  1. Find the customer: scroll, or type a name or vehicle number in the search box. Each customer shows what they owe and how much of their limit is used.
  2. Choose the vehicle. Use "No number / container" if there is none.
  3. Choose the fuel: HS diesel, MS petrol, X2 premium, XG green, or CNG (sold in kg).
  4. Choose Amount (₹) or Litres (kg for CNG), and type the figure from the dispenser. The line under the box shows the rate, litres and total, so check it matches.
  5. Press Record credit sale. A slip appears: check the amount and give the customer the signed slip.
The rate is automatic (today's IOC price; CNG at the pump price the owner sets). If a rate looks wrong or says "no current rate", do not sell on credit: tell the owner.
Mistake? Do not enter the sale again. Tell the owner the customer, amount and time so it can be corrected. A suspended account cannot be chosen: the owner decides about credit for it.

2. Payment received

Payment tab, or the Payment button on Home.

  1. Choose the customer (the list shows what they owe).
  2. Type the amount actually received.
  3. Choose the mode: Cash, Cheque or Bank Transfer. For a cheque or transfer, type the cheque number or UTR as the reference.
  4. Press Record payment.
  • Cash and cheque reduce the customer's balance straight away.
  • Bank transfer is held as "pending". The balance changes only after the owner sees the money in the bank and confirms it. Until then it shows under "Pending action" on Home.
  • If a customer pays more than they owe, the extra is kept as credit in their favour. Nothing is lost.

3. Expense

Expense tab, or the Expense button on Home. Do it every day an expense is paid; Home shows a reminder until one is logged.

  1. Type the amount and choose the category (Staff, Maintenance, Utilities, Supplies, Misc).
  2. Add a short description if it helps, for example "Diesel generator fuel".
  3. Check the date, then press Log expense.

4. Lube Sale

Lube tab, or the Lube Sale button on Home.

  1. Choose the product. The price fills in; change the unit price only if the sale was really at another price.
  2. Type the quantity.
  3. Choose Cash or Credit. For Credit, choose the customer: the amount is added to what they owe.
  4. Press Log sale.

A lube product that is not in the list has no price set yet. Ask the owner. A sale below the cost price is recorded, but the owner is alerted.
